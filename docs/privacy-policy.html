BellForge Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Quant Design LLC

BellForge ("the app") is made by Quant Design LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: you can use BellForge without an account, and then everything stays on your phone. If you create an account, we store what's needed for friends, challenges and backup, and nothing else. We don't sell your information or show ads.

Using BellForge without an account

The app saves the following on your phone so it can work:

  • Your first name, level, weekly goal and equipment settings
  • Workouts you complete, your streak, personal bests, saved workouts, program progress and challenges
  • Meal plan inputs you enter, such as age, height, weight, activity level, goal, diet and allergies

Without an account, this information stays in the app's storage on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

If you create an account

Accounts are optional. When you create one, we store:

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored as a secure hash, never readable by us), your username, display name and level.
  • Friends: who you've sent friend requests to, received requests from, and accepted.
  • Activity: short updates when you finish a workout or start or join a challenge, such as the workout name, minutes trained and time.
  • Challenges: challenges you create or join, their rules and dates, and your score.
  • Backup: a copy of your workout history, streak, saved workouts, program progress, challenges and app settings, so you can restore them on a new phone. Meal plan inputs (age, height, weight, allergies and so on) are not backed up; they stay on your phone.

We use this information only to run these features. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it.

Who can see your information

  • Anyone signed in to BellForge can look up your username and display name, so friends can find and add you.
  • Friends you accept can see your activity updates and the challenges you start.
  • People in a challenge with you can see your name and score in that challenge.
  • Only you can see your email address and your backup.

Where it's stored

Account information is stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the United States. It is encrypted in transit, and access rules limit each person to their own data and what their friends share. Supabase processes it only on our behalf.

Information we don't collect

BellForge has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and it does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or health data from other apps.

Sharing outside the app

When you tap Invite friends or Share your workout, your phone's share menu opens and you choose where the message goes. We don't see or store what you send.

Links to other sites

The gear shop opens links to third-party stores, such as Amazon, in your phone's browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle your information.

Beta testing services

If you install a test version through Google Play testing or Apple TestFlight, that service may collect information such as your email address, device model and app version to deliver the build and its feedback tools. This is handled under Google's or Apple's privacy policies.

Deleting your information

Your account: open the app, go to Progress → Your account → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, username, friends, activity, challenge scores and backup from our database. You can also email development@quant-design.com from the address on your account and we'll delete it within 30 days. More details: How to delete your BellForge account.

Your phone: uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings to remove what's stored on your device.

We keep account information until you delete your account.

Children

BellForge is intended for adults 18 and over and is not directed at children. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we will delete it.

Health information

The app's calorie and protein targets are estimates for general fitness and are not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise or nutrition program.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before adding features that collect new kinds of information, such as notifications or real meal orders. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
